# QC Report 2: Teaching Quality & Instructional Design Review

**Verdict:** Needs revision | **Confidence:** Evaluated against 45-minute bell-to-bell instructional design. Objective coverage is strong; pacing in the practical activity is slightly ambitious.

**What Was Verified:** Checked Bloom's taxonomy alignment for GET/POST differentiation and status code classification. All 3 objective parts are explicitly taught and tested.

### Pedagogical Recommendations Table

| Issue Found | Suggested Improvement | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Practical activity]** The activity asks students to inspect 6 distinct network requests in 7 minutes, which leaves under 70 seconds per case for paired discussion. | Reduce the guided activity from 6 scenarios to 3 focused cases (one 200, one 404, one 500), and move the remaining three to extension practice. | MEDIUM | Pending |
| **[Introduction]** The term 'payload' is introduced in the first 2 minutes before being conceptually defined. | Add a simple 1-sentence lay definition: 'payload (the data package being carried)' when first mentioned. | LOW | Pending |
